# Edit combos

Techniques that are always used together, bundled so they are applied as one decision. Each lists its parts (see [motion-toolkit.md](../craft-ae/components.md)), when to use it, timing, and the check that tells you it failed.

| Combo | Parts | Use when | Timing | Failure check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hook reveal | Photo or footage card of the subject + headline with the surprising fact + push-in toward the key detail | First shot | Card in at 0, headline by 0.5 s, push over 3–5 s | Viewer cannot tell what the object is in 1 s |
| Proof cut | Claim line → real footage card within 1 s + credit line; two cuts of the same demo if the action is longer than 4 s | Any "it can do X" claim | Cut lands on the claim's last word | Footage shows something else, or an AI illustration is used as proof |
| Concept beat | Blurred same-source background + keyword stack (one accent word) | Naming the parts of a mechanism | Words 0.38 s apart, under 2 s total | More than three words, or words not spoken |
| Annotated demo | Footage card + chips at cue times (+ highlight ring when a label sits inside the footage) | Explaining a step while showing it | Each chip on its narration word | Chips cover the action, or appear before the words |
| Diagram focus | Card of the author's or official diagram cropped to the relevant part + highlight ring | Explaining a data path or architecture | Ring at 0.7 s | Diagram contradicts the current version being described |
| Graphic-only beat | `bg` mode with the same source blurred + statement / struck icon / phone pair + accent chip for the takeaway | No real footage exists for a necessary point | Main graphic by 0.5 s, takeaway chip after the key word | Graphic implies real behavior it cannot prove |
| Limits beat | Evidence crop (log, settings, docs) as a small card + stacked limit chips | Costs and boundaries | One chip per limit, on its word | Limits read as a long list; more than three chips |
| Closing question | Footage card dimmed by scrim + two-line question (second line accent) + credits + 0.6 s fade to black | Final shot | Question by 0.9 s, credits by 1.6 s | Credits overlap captions or the source line |

## Sequencing

- These combos say what each shot does. For a specific problem inside a shot or across the film (weak opening, flat middle, rough joins), pick from `choose.md` and note the technique in the shot.

- Alternate combos: hook reveal → proof cut → concept beat → annotated demo / diagram focus → limits beat → closing question. Avoid two graphic-only beats in a row.
- Reuse one source across non-adjacent shots only with different ranges or crops.
- When a combo needs material that does not exist, return to video-material-scout before substituting a graphic.
