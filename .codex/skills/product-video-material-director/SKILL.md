# Product video material director

Own the image-to-usable-clip decisions for each product shot. Use the user's selected category, presentation style and enabled stage methods; they are independent of Persona identity. Do not silently load all method cards or turn one sample's style, shot count or duration into a requirement.

The platform owns product facts, references, immutable revisions, selected methods and review states. The executing Harness uses this Skill to make creative decisions. Follow [video-production-handoff](../video-production-handoff/SKILL.md) for exact platform CLI/MCP, frozen execution, manual website, upload and acceptance contracts. User authorization comes from the current task, never this Skill.

## Establish the material's job

Read the intended buyer situation, one purchase reason, exact product source material, selected category/style revisions and this shot's purpose. State what new information this shot adds. Distinguish a scene illustration from genuine evidence. A generated operation cannot establish real comfort, load capacity, waterproofing, assembly speed or dimensions.

When the supplied product view cannot support a requested angle, internal detail or real demonstration, identify the needed reference. A limited concept or appearance scene remains possible if clearly described. Do not improve the product's appearance by altering its actual structure, accessories or markings.

## Build and review the keyframe

Use genuine product references in the actual image-generation input, retaining their exact source and hashes. Compose the frame for its intended action: product visible, useful viewing angle, enough space for movement and an understandable scene. Save the image-generation description separately from motion instructions.

Inspect actual output pixels against references in two passes:
- Product accuracy: silhouette, structure, visible color, proportions, accessories and markings. Unknown details remain unknown.
- Visual and motion suitability: framing, lighting, clutter, contact/occlusion, subject anatomy, plausible movement space and continuity with neighboring shots.

Do not accept attractive but inaccurate images. Save observations and the candidate revision/hash; show the user the actual keyframe and obtain exact selection through the platform. Library acceptance alone is not selection for this shot. Existing explicit user selection remains valid for the same bytes; changing the image requires new selection.

## Design motion from the selected image

Actually inspect the selected image through an available image-capable model or Harness view. A filename, image prompt or earlier image version is insufficient. Record the actual image observation and exact revision/hash. If image inspection is unavailable, explain that gap and keep the prompt unverified.

Use the shot's job and visible image to plan one understandable action. Describe:
- Details and spatial relationships that must stay stable.
- Subject action, direction and start/end state.
- Camera framing, movement and what it should reveal.
- Pacing and timing aligned with the intended edit range.
- Lighting continuity and specific failure constraints.

Separate camera motion from product motion. Prefer a feasible short action; split incompatible movements or complicated operations into distinct shots. Do not invent hidden product surfaces or expect a static image to demonstrate unverified real-world performance. Avoid contradictory camera instructions and vague adjective lists.

Produce a distinct motion prompt adapted to the user's selected Grok/Gemini website. Give requested aspect, generation duration, intended use duration and audio intent without claiming unavailable website settings are supported. Keep typography, prices and exact parameters for verified postproduction. A strong prompt improves direction but does not establish output quality.

## Handoff and returned-clip review

Save/confirm the prompt against the exact selected image, then prepare the existing manual website task. The user downloads that reference, pastes the prompt, generates at the website and returns the original MP4. Do not operate the website or generate on the user's behalf when the agreed route is manual.

Associate the returned file with its exact shot/task and hash. Inspect actual video frames/motion and available audio, stating the inspection method and any unobserved modality. Review product consistency, deformation, anatomy/contact, flicker, camera/action compliance, continuity and potentially usable edit ranges. Add timestamps for visible defects. File validity and prompt compliance are separate from creative approval; upload is separate from acceptance and saved Master adoption.

Trace a failed result back to the image, action feasibility, ambiguous instructions, supplier noncompliance or chosen edit range. Recommend a specific change at the responsible step, retaining original attempts. A previously accepted execution stays terminal; revisions become new attempts/tasks under the platform contract.

Only explicitly selected feedback becomes a reusable category/style/stage preference candidate. Label untested fixes unverified. A correction to one product's dimensions never becomes a category fact. Preserve enabled-method switches and exact revisions.

Deliver the reviewed clip and use-range recommendation to the normal narration/music, DaVinci main edit and AE packaging flow. This Skill's completion is a reviewed material handoff, not a completed long-form edit or public publication.
