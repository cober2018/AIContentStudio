## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Music goes to the BGM folder of the common library

When an audio material carrying music information is filed into the common library, it SHALL be placed under 音频/BGM; other audio SHALL stay under 音频/音效.

#### Scenario: Filing a tagged track
- **WHEN** the track Carefree, which has music scene and mood information, is filed into the common library
- **THEN** its file lands in 通用素材库/音频/BGM
