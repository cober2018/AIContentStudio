## Decisions

- **A deal wraps a project**, it does not replace it: production, review and delivery stay the same flows, so brand work gets the same quality controls.
- **Brand approval is a recorded external review**, separate from the user's own approval; nothing is sent to brands automatically.
- **Ad-law check is a rule check**, not a model judgment, with a maintained term list and per-platform label rules.
- **Money is tracked, not processed**: no payments are made through the platform.
