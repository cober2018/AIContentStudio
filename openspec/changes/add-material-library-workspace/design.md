## Decisions

- **Derivatives are cache, not truth.** Thumbnails and posters live under a cache folder keyed by content hash; deleting them is safe; linked drive files are read, never written.
- **One page per job.** Materials (media you put in a piece) are separated from knowledge (documents and evidence you rely on).
- **Unavailable drive materials still list** with their cached thumbnail and an "unavailable" badge.
